# Border Force — the lean shift

Two officers, one bench, a belt of suitcases going round. Open a bag, lay out
what is inside, seize what is forbidden today, leave everything else.

## The pieces

A hundred and thirty-one punch-out pieces, fifty-one designs: three knives,
four lighters, four screwdrivers, and three each of five hats, five t-shirts,
five trousers, five dresses, five tote bags, five bottles, five pairs of
underwear and five books. Sixteen suitcases of eight, so three ordinary pieces
stay in the box each game — never a knife, lighter or screwdriver.

**Every piece has two sides**, and either side can get it confiscated. A piece
is packed whichever way up it landed — sometimes back up — and it stays that
way up until somebody turns it over, for the rest of the game. Tap a piece to
turn it over. Pieces also come out of the bag **at any angle**, a fresh one
every time the bag is opened.

## The wall

Twenty-one signs in a grid seven across and three down, **shuffled into a new
layout every game**: Knives, Lighters, Screwdrivers, Hats, T-shirts, Trousers,
Dresses, Tote bags, Bottles, Books, Underwear, Instruments, Belts, Teddies,
Smiley faces, Balls, and the colours Green, White, Red, Yellow and Black.

Knives, Lighters and Screwdrivers start red; everything else starts green. A
piece is forbidden if anything on either side of it matches a red sign.

**The wall never turns by itself.** It only moves when somebody uses a tool.

## Tools

Knives, lighters and screwdrivers score like anything else when seized while
their sign is red, and they also go into your hand. Use them before the next
bag.

- **Screwdriver** (four). Turn any one sign over: green to red, or red to
  green.
- **Lighter** (four). Strike it on a red sign. That sign and every red sign
  joined to it — above, below or either side, as far as the red runs — goes
  back to green.
- **Knife** (three). Stab any sign. Nothing can turn it this round, and fire
  stops at it.

Before each bag, Officer B stabs first, then you use your knife, lighter and
screwdriver in that order, then B uses theirs.

## Scoring and the end

Each forbidden piece you seize scores 1. Seize something legal and you sit out
the next bag.

The game ends when either:
- somebody reaches **16** (or 20 in the longer game), or
- the wall has **no red sign at all** — however that happens, by lighter or
  screwdriver. Whoever has seized more wins, and equal is a dead heat. Burning
  the last of the red while you are ahead wins the game.

## What counts as what

- **Smiley faces:** the beanie and the blue dress show them on the front only;
  the black tote shows them on both sides.
- **Teddies:** the white dress, front only.
- **Balls:** the tennis tote, front only.
- **Instruments:** the guitar t-shirt, and the piano book, front only.
- **Colours:**
  - The red wine counts as red.
  - The yellow book is the only book with a colour.
  - The blue thong, the blue tote, the rum and the water have no colour.
